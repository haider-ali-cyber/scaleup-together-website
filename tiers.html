Important Notes on Profit Sharing

Profit sharing applies to net-new trading profit only. Exact terms — including high-water mark calculation, treatment of losing periods, fee crystallization period, trading costs, and withdrawal terms — are defined in your individual client agreement and reviewed with you during your private consultation, before any funds are committed.
