About Us

Built on Discipline, Not Hype

ScaleUp Together is a professional portfolio and Forex account management service built around one idea: your capital deserves a disciplined, transparent process — not promises.

Our Approach

A more disciplined way to approach the markets

We combine market experience, structured risk management and active portfolio oversight to create a professional approach to global markets — for clients who want disciplined management rather than speculative promises.

ScaleUp Together is led by founder Itrut Malik, who brings 20+ years of experience navigating global Forex and financial markets. That depth of experience is the foundation of our approach — disciplined market participation, structured risk management and long-term client relationships, built on one simple principle: we only succeed when you do.

For clients who have struggled to achieve consistent results in Forex, our account management service offers a structured alternative. Our trading team manages your Forex account through disciplined strategies, active risk management and continuous market oversight, while your capital remains in your own broker account.

Your capital. Your account. Our expertise.
