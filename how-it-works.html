How ScaleUp Together works

A straightforward, transparent path from first consultation to active management — five steps, always the same process.

01

Consultation

We start with a private, no-obligation conversation to discuss your objectives, capital, and risk appetite — and determine whether ScaleUp Together is the right fit for you.

02

Account Setup

You open your own designated broker account, under your name. Your capital never passes through ScaleUp Together directly.

03

Trading Authorization

You provide agreed, trading-only access to your account through your broker's authorization structure. You retain full ownership, visibility, and control at all times.

04

Active Management

We actively manage trading activity within your chosen plan's structure, applying disciplined risk management and continuous monitoring.

05

Performance Review

We review results with you on a regular basis, discuss ongoing strategy, and ensure full transparency into account activity and performance.
